A virtual leads group that still feels local

Meet online every week in 45 minutes, without the drive or the breakfast bill. Meet in person once a month, so the names you trade become people you know.

Why virtual weekly

A weekly meeting is what builds trust, and the commute is what kills attendance. Moving the weekly exchange online keeps the rhythm and drops the hour on the road.

Cameras stay on, pods stay small, and every minute has a job, from ask rounds to Power Pair huddles.

Wins5 min

Hot seat10 min

Why in person monthly

Some trust only happens over a handshake. Each group picks a monthly spot and rotates it, so every part of town gets a turn hosting.

Questions people ask

Do virtual leads groups work as well as in-person ones?

They work when the format is tight and attendance is consistent. Ours pairs a 45-minute weekly video exchange with an optional monthly in-person meetup.

What do I need to join a virtual exchange?

A phone or computer with a camera and a quiet place for 45 minutes. The link is posted in your group room.
