Fort Collins leads group

Northern Colorado's referral group. Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor, Timnath, Wellington and Greeley in one room each week, with one seat per trade.

Claim your seatSee open seats

Who this group serves

Our founder lived in Fort Collins for five years and went to Colorado State University before leaving to build a business, so this group is personal. Northern Colorado has grown fast, and new residents need a plumber, a realtor and a dentist they can trust. A group whose members already know each other is how those newcomers find the right people.

Cities in this group: Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor, Timnath, Wellington, Johnstown, Berthoud, Greeley, Severance and Estes Park.

How the Fort Collins group runs

  • 45-minute virtual exchange every week, at a time the founders choose
  • Optional in-person meetup once a month
  • One Group Captain, with our system handling agenda, reminders and scoring
  • A private group room on allofus.one for messages between meetings

Local small business, by the numbers

Northern Colorado in context

The SBA reports 730,887 small businesses across Colorado, and 48.6% of the state's employees work at one. Its 2025 major-metro profiles do not include the Fort Collins metro, so we do not publish a local count we cannot source.

Source: U.S. Small Business Administration, Office of Advocacy, 2025 Small Business Profiles (Census Nonemployer Statistics 2022 and Statistics of U.S. Businesses 2022).

Fort Collins questions

Does the Fort Collins group cover Greeley and Loveland?

Yes. If your customers are anywhere in Northern Colorado, this is your group. A dedicated Greeley group can form once enough owners there ask for one.

Why is there no local business chart on this page?

The SBA's 2025 major-metro profiles cover Denver and Colorado Springs but not the Fort Collins metro, and we only publish numbers we can source.

Can CSU-related businesses join?

Yes. Any business with customers in Northern Colorado can claim a seat, as long as its trade is still open.
