What a new group needs

  • One Group Captain who runs the weekly exchange
  • Four to six founding members in different trades
  • A weekly time the founders agree on
  • A spot for the monthly in-person meetup

Colorado questions

Which Colorado groups are open?

Denver Metro, Colorado Springs and Fort Collins are forming now. Each one holds one seat per trade.

Can I start a group in another Colorado town?

Yes. If you have a few business owners ready to go in Boulder, Pueblo, Grand Junction or anywhere else, apply to start a new group and we will help you launch it.

When do Colorado groups meet?

Each group meets online for 45 minutes every week. The founding members of each group vote on the day and time, and each group also meets in person once a month.
