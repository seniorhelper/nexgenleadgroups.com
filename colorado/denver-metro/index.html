Denver Metro leads group

One seat per trade across the Denver metro. Meet online every week, meet in person once a month, and trade leads with a Power Pair who serves the same customers you do.

Claim your seatSee open seats

Who this group serves

NexGen Lead Groups is headquartered in downtown Denver, so this is our home group. The metro stretches from Brighton to Castle Rock and from Golden to Aurora, which is exactly why the weekly exchange is online. Nobody loses an hour on I-25 to trade leads, and the monthly meetup moves around the metro so every side of town gets a turn.

Cities in this group: Denver, Aurora, Lakewood, Arvada, Westminster, Thornton, Northglenn, Commerce City, Brighton, Broomfield, Centennial, Littleton, Englewood, Highlands Ranch, Parker, Castle Rock and Golden.

How the Denver Metro group runs

  • 45-minute virtual exchange every week, at a time the founders choose
  • Optional in-person meetup once a month
  • One Group Captain, with our system handling agenda, reminders and scoring
  • A private group room on allofus.one for messages between meetings

Denver Metro questions

Where does the Denver Metro group meet in person?

The monthly meetup rotates around the metro. The Group Captain posts each location in the group room on allofus.one a few weeks ahead.

Can I join if my business is in Boulder or Golden?

Yes. If your customers are in the metro, you belong here. Boulder owners can also start a Boulder group of their own.
