How NexGen leads groups work

A leads group built around the moments that create customers, the partners who see those moments first, and tools that keep leads moving between meetings.

Claim one seat per trade

Each group holds one seat per trade, so nobody in the room competes with you for a referral.

Write your trigger moments

Three to five plain-language moments that make someone need you. Your group learns them the first week.

Get matched into a Power Pair

The system ranks every member by shared customers and shared trigger moments, then suggests your strongest partner.

Work the week

Use the Idea Machine and Who Do I Know to find leads for your partner, and hand them off with a Lead Card.

Meet for 45 minutes

Wins, ask rounds, pair huddles and a hot seat. Then everyone goes back to work.

Why trigger moments beat a speech

"I do insurance" is easy to forget. "Call me when someone you know just bought a car or a house" is easy to act on. Trigger moments turn every member into someone who knows exactly when to think of you.

The Idea Machine writes a starting set for 60 trades. You edit them into your own words.

Questions people ask

How long is the weekly meeting?

45 minutes: 5 minutes of wins, 15 minutes of ask rounds in pods, 15 minutes of Power Pair huddles and a 10-minute hot seat.

What is a trigger moment?

A moment in a customer's life that makes them need you, like buying a home, opening a business or getting engaged. Members learn each other's trigger moments so they know exactly when to make an introduction.

How often can I change my Power Pair?

Every week if you want. Many pairs stay together for months, but nobody is stuck with a match that is not producing.

What does the Group Captain do?

The Captain runs the weekly exchange, welcomes guests and keeps the group room friendly. The system handles the agenda, reminders and the contribution score.
